## NUMBERED LIST RULE

If the topic contains a number (e.g. "7 bangles", "5 tips", "10 products"):

- Write EXACTLY that many items
- Each item gets its OWN H2 or H3 section
- Each section has a full descriptive heading + full paragraph

**Example — Topic: "5 Oxidised Bangles Under ₹500"**

Never bury all items in one paragraph.

---

## PARAGRAPH RULES

Every paragraph must:
- Have **4 to 5 connected sentences** — not 1, not 2, not 3
- Flow naturally as prose — not bullet points
- Not start with "Additionally", "Furthermore", "Moreover"

## LANGUAGE RULES

Write for a regular Indian reader on their phone. Simple, clear, friendly.

- Maximum 14 words per sentence
- Class 8 reading level — if a school student wouldn't understand it, rewrite it
- Use ₹ for all prices (not Rs. or INR)
- Use Indian festivals and occasions as examples (Diwali, Raksha Bandhan, Navratri, Karva Chauth)
- Use Indian contexts (office, college, wedding, daily wear, gifting)

**Replace these immediately:**
| Remove | Use instead |
|--------|------------|
| "auspicious" | "special" or "lucky" |
| "leverage" | "use" |
| "utilize" | "use" |
| "fundamentally" | "completely" |
| "characterized by" | "known for" |
| "In today's world" | (delete — just start the point) |
| "It is important to note" | (delete) |
| "In conclusion" | (delete — write a real closing line) |
| "seamlessly" | (delete) |
| "delve into" | (delete) |

**Add at least 2 of these naturally:**
- *"Most people don't know this…"*
- *"This is where many buyers go wrong…"*
- *"Here's the honest answer…"*
- *"If you remember just one thing from this, remember this…"*

## PRICE LOCK RULES

The brand's PRICE RANGE from Step 1 is a hard limit.

**Example:** Brand max is ₹800

| Scenario | Correct action |
|----------|---------------|
| Writing "under ₹2,000" | Change to "under ₹800" |
| Writing "₹500 to ₹5,000" | Change to "₹200 to ₹700" |
| Writing "premium options at ₹10,000" | Remove completely |
| Gift guide categories | Max budget tier = brand's max price |

## CONTENT TYPE STRUCTURES

### Buying Guide
1. What the buyer is confused about (hook)
2. What to check before buying
3. Best options by category or price bracket
4. Who should pick which option
5. Quick buying tips
6. FAQ
7. Final recommendation + CTA

### Educational Post
1. Hook — the question most people have
2. Simple explanation of the concept
3. Why it matters practically
4. Types or varieties
5. Common mistakes to avoid
6. FAQ
7. Soft product mention + CTA

### Gift Guide
1. The occasion and why it matters
2. Budget categories (e.g. Under ₹300 / Under ₹500 / Under ₹800)
3. Best picks per budget
4. Who each gift is for
5. Quick gifting tips
6. FAQ
7. CTA — where to buy

### Styling Tips
1. The style challenge readers face
2. Why this product is versatile
3. 3-4 outfit or occasion combinations
4. What to avoid
5. FAQ
6. CTA

### Product Care
1. Why care matters
2. Most common mistakes
3. Step-by-step care routine
4. Dos and Don'ts
5. FAQ
6. CTA

---

## TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

- **Word count:** 800–1,100 words — tight, no filler
- **Bold:** One important line per section (the most useful insight)
- **FAQ:** 3–4 questions minimum
- **Closing:** Must end with a complete paragraph + soft CTA — not "In conclusion"
- **Spacing:** One blank line after every heading, one blank line between every paragraph

---
