# SEO Framework Reference

## Algorithm Context (2026)

Every blog written with this skill must be calibrated to:

- **March 2026 Core Update** — content depth, cross-reference signal, Core Web Vitals weight
- **December 2025 E-E-A-T expansion** — E-E-A-T now applies to ALL verticals, not just YMYL
- **February 2026 Discover Core Update** — locally relevant content prioritization
- **AI Overviews** — appear in ~25% of all Google searches as of Q2 2026

---

## E-E-A-T Principles to Apply

**Experience** — Write like someone who has actually used the products. Use first-person anecdotes, real usage scenarios, specific situations Indian buyers face.

**Expertise** — Show product knowledge. Explain the difference between types, materials, occasions. Don't be vague.

**Authoritativeness** — Reference real product names, real price ranges, real festivals and occasions. Avoid unverifiable statistics.

**Trustworthiness** — Never invent prices, never exaggerate quality claims, never write fake reviews or testimonials.

---

## GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)

Write blogs that AI systems can extract and cite cleanly:

- Start every major section with a direct, clear answer (40-60 words)
- Use declarative sentence structures: "Silver bangles last longer than gold-plated ones because..."
- Include definition-first paragraphs where relevant
- FAQ sections are especially valuable for AI Overview eligibility

## Indian D2C Market Context

**Price Sensitivity:**
Indian D2C buyers are highly price-conscious. Always include specific ₹ price points. Vague "affordable" language does not convert.

**Mobile-First Reading:**
Most Indian readers discover blogs on mobile. Short sentences, clear headings, easy-to-skim format. Dense academic paragraphs get abandoned immediately.

**Festival Calendar (major triggers):**
- January: Makar Sankranti, Republic Day, Lohri
- February: Valentine's Day, Basant Panchami
- March: Holi, International Women's Day
- April: Ugadi, Baisakhi, Tamil New Year
- May: Mother's Day, Akshaya Tritiya
- June–July: Monsoon gifting, Eid
- August: Raksha Bandhan, Independence Day, Janmashtami
- September: Ganesh Chaturthi, Teacher's Day
- October: Navratri, Durga Puja, Dussehra
- November: Diwali, Dhanteras, Bhai Dooj
- December: Christmas, New Year gifting

**Top Content Formats for Indian D2C:**
1. Number-led listicles ("7 bangles under ₹500")
2. Festival gift guides
3. "How to style" guides
4. "Before you buy" guides
5. Care and maintenance posts

## Quality Standards

A blog fails if it:
- Uses wrong prices for the brand
- Suggests wrong industry products
- Has poem-style one-sentence-per-line paragraphs
- Has generic H2 headings ("Introduction", "Conclusion")
- Is under 750 words or over 1,200 words
- Has no FAQ section
- Ends abruptly without a CTA
- Uses banned filler phrases ("In today's world", "In conclusion")
