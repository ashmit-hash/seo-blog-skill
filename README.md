# SEO Blog Generator — Claude Skill

**Generate a complete, SEO-optimized blog post for any Indian D2C brand website — directly inside Claude.**

One command. Full blog. Real products. Correct prices. Ready to publish.

---

## What This Does

This is a **Claude Code skill** — a plugin that teaches Claude a complete SEO blog generation workflow.

Once installed, type:

And Claude will:

1. **Scrape your website live** — reads your homepage, detects your niche and price range
2. **Suggest 5 blog topics** — you pick the one you want
3. **Write a full 800–1,100 word blog** — with your real products and correct prices
4. **Deliver the SEO package** — meta title, description, URL slug, keywords
5. **Revise on demand** — request changes until you're happy, then approve

No paid tools. No API keys. No web app. Just Claude and your website URL.

---

## What Makes This Different

### 🔒 Price Lock
Reads your website's actual product prices before writing. A brand selling under ₹800 will **never** get a blog suggesting ₹5,000 examples.

### 🔒 Niche Lock
17 industry patterns prevent cross-niche mistakes. Candle brands get candle content. Food brands get food content.

### 🛍️ Real Product Names
Reads your product names from the live website and weaves them naturally into the blog.

### ✍️ Paragraph Quality
Every paragraph is checked for 4–5 connected sentences. No one-sentence-per-line output.

### ✅ Self-Checking Output
Before presenting the blog, Claude runs a quality checklist and fixes anything that fails.
